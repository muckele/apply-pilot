# Offline document writing candidate, prompt/cache v12

This candidate builds on published `c4616f450d8dcc578fd0be85235512c214b24bf2`. It changes instructions and deterministic writing checks; the provider response schemas, standalone fact grammar, source ownership/qualifiers/negation checks and job-only attestation authority remain the same. No migration or new factual authority is introduced. Receipt persistence is documented separately in `CORRECTION_FLOW_SAFE_RECEIPTS.md`.

Résumé emphasis now includes server-published conditional within-record ordering opportunities, bound into the system prompt and request hash. For each typed work/project record with an unambiguous source header and complete standalone bullet facts, the proxy counts distinct non-generic exact words shared with a single stated requirement, preference or technology criterion. Only when a later bullet has at least two shared words and beats the source's first bullet does it require a top-scoring fact first in that same record. Ties, already relevant source order, low overlap and ambiguous source blocks acquire no rule. The check uses citation-bound source facts, so a verb substitution or a duplicate in the summary cannot count as prioritization. All other facts and record chronology remain intact. For the fixed service-operations example, the reporting/SQL outcome moves before general onboarding within Fabrikam; every record, date and fact remains present.

This is a transparent lexical proxy, not semantic evaluation or an employer score. It cannot understand every synonym, decide the strongest career argument or establish human acceptance. It enforces ownership and ordering only inside the published, unambiguous opportunity records; it does not prove global record chronology or all other layout relationships. The prompt requires preserving record chronology, and owner review must check it. A general document structure validator is outside this bounded writing correction. The model still receives the complete job and fact catalog; owner review still decides usefulness. The candidate does not require gratuitous edits when the source already leads with relevant evidence.

The letter prompt now leads with the applicant's evidence and groups related complete first-person sentences around two priorities. Repeated requirement lead-ins, duplicate fact recital and bare action fragments fail quality checks. When at least four action facts are selected, at least two paragraphs must each contain two complete action sentences. A required job-only attestation can remain separate, and noun-only limited sources are not forced into unsupported invented sentences. Existing minimum evidence/source-family checks remain. The authored positive examples are fixed independently of the ranking implementation; negative fixtures include source-copy/synonym controls, requirement recital, fragments, isolated sentences, repeated evidence, ownership transfer, changed quantities/status and invented causal bridges.

For example, source facts `Directed service delivery across support, engineering, and customer success for 42 enterprise accounts.` and `Reduced median incident review time from five business days to two by standardizing evidence capture and ownership.` become two separately cited sentences in one paragraph: `I directed service delivery across support, engineering, and customer success for 42 enterprise accounts. I reduced median incident review time from five business days to two by standardizing evidence capture and ownership.` No combined fact, new employer attribution, causal bridge or qualification is inferred.

Free connective prose, new causal explanations and synthesized qualifications remain outside the strict factual grammar. Better flow comes from selection, ordering, supported first-person prefixes and paragraph grouping. A materially freer writing architecture would need its own evidence contract and review rather than weakening these checks.

Both generation features use prompt/cache version **12**. Existing v11 cached outputs cannot satisfy the new versioned request. The 70-fact synthetic source and reviewed-evidence payload are unchanged; version-bound catalog and request hashes are refreshed explicitly in diagnostic fixtures. Model `gemini-3.8-flash`, LOW thinking, token/spending ceilings, no retries/fallbacks, sequential failure stops, actual PDF/DOCX verification and owner review remain unchanged. No paid attempt is authorized by this candidate. The lost c4616f4 receipt and its actual decisions/usage/cost remain unknown.
