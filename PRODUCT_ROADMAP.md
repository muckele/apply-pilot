# Product Roadmap

## Delivered in the Private MVP

- Review-before-save Chrome extension for manual job capture
- Application Answer Vault with copy-only handoff
- Live resume preview and ATS-friendly DOCX/PDF formatting controls
- Deterministic job filtering followed by capped AI ranking
- Interview question and STAR story library
- AI usage, model, discovery, and budget controls
- Account data export and delete-my-data controls
- Recruiter Gmail triage and save-to-CRM workflow

## Recommended Next Features

- Calendar integration for interview reminders
- Salary comparison and offer comparison
- Company research brief
- Recruiter contact and networking CRM
- Referral tracker
- LinkedIn profile optimization checklist
- Weekly job-search plan and saved search alerts
- Application quality score and resume A/B testing
- Follow-up cadence automation with explicit approval gates
- AI mock interview mode
- Kanban pipeline board
- Data export to CSV and scheduled backups
- Mobile-friendly PWA
- Duplicate company detection

## Suggested Milestones

1. Deploy and observe the private MVP with real job-search usage.
2. Add calendar reminders, company research, and offer comparison.
3. Add mock interview practice and application quality analytics.
4. Add PWA support, saved alerts, and optional private object storage.
